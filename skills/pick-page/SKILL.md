# Pick pages

The author decides on previews. A pick page shows the current state (**Now**) beside numbered **takes**; the author marks them and sends a **pick line**; nothing gets built before a pick. Round by round the takes grow into a tree: a take improves into its next version (2 → 2.1), branches off into new ones (4 → 4A), combines with others, or drops out.

**Profile first.** If the project has `.claude/pick-profile.md` (older projects: `.claude/study-profile.md`), read it before anything else: what to read about the project, where pages go, the project's look rules, how to check pages on this machine, and what follows a pick. Its rules add to this skill and win where they differ.

## A. Making a page (a new one, or its next round)

1. **Read what exists now.** The subject's docs, code and data, its earlier pages, and whatever shares the screen or the system with it, so the takes fit beside it. Take every number (colours, sizes, rates, costs, timings) from those files. Fix any stale doc line you find. The page's About says in a few lines what the subject does, how often, and what sits next to it.
2. **Plan the takes.** Each take is really different: another design, shape or approach. Variants of one idea are one take with a slider. Order them from close to bold, put Now first, and star (★) the one I'd pick with its reason. Write each caption from the take's visible source first: what it starts from, then what it does.
   - **Cut the subject first** (author, 12.11): list its pieces (moments, objects on screen, aspects a take changes) and give each one home by [reference/parts.md](reference/parts.md). A piece that can be swapped between takes and that the game builds apart gets its own tab, a part of a complex when the thing has others; one the author judges apart that can't be swapped gets a row (`layers`) on every take of the tab, on looks, mechanics and UI as on sounds.
   - **Complexes** (author, 4.11): when tabs are parts of one thing in the game (a weapon's shoot, flight and hit sounds; a boss's body, each skill's look and its sound), put them in one complex (`in`, `complexes`: [reference/shell.md](reference/shell.md#complexes)) and say in the About how they connect. Each tab then shows the whole thing with its part lit, in step on the complex's clock. **A complex always has its `lanes` (every part's sounds with their times, one line per sound) and one preview** that plays every part at its pick or last-shown take; tabs grouped with `in` and an About alone are not a complex, and the self-test fails them (author, 4.11 evening). A part that depends on another adapts to it where it can (a sound timed to the look's hit); where it can't be designed before the other is picked, it waits (`waiting`) and gets its takes next round. Parts already picked on other pages join as `locked`.
3. **Write the source** in `src/study.js` beside the page, following [reference/shell.md](reference/shell.md) (the STUDY format, painters, controls, sliders, sounds). For a next round, follow [reference/pick-line.md](reference/pick-line.md) "Making the next round".
   - **Words** (author, 7.11: «I am NOT reading all of this»): the author reads headlines. Every block is a headline that says the point, then at most three short lines in plain words. Code, dates and sound recipes stay off the page, in comments in `src/`. [reference/words.md](reference/words.md) has the limits; the self-test counts them, and the checker's `--words` lists every block over them in seconds.
4. **Build and check.** Build with the shell's build script; run the self-test to `errors=0`; look at the page at desktop and phone width. A page passes when the self-test is clean and every take draws at both widths. The phone frame check runs only on takes whose drawing is new or changed, and is skipped when no drawing changed ([reference/shell.md](reference/shell.md#checking-a-page)). The skill's `checker/` runs all of it (setup once per PC: `checker/README.md`). Checks are silent: the author is at the same computer, so launch any headless browser muted (Chromium/Edge `--mute-audio`) and never press ▶ Play in a visible browser; check a clip by decoding it (`decodeAudioData`), not by playing it.
5. **Hand it over.** Publish the page as a private artifact (a next round republishes to the same one). Give the author the link and the file path, and in 3–6 lines what is on the page and what is still open. Stop there: the next move is the author's pick line.

## B. When a pick line arrives

Read [reference/pick-line.md](reference/pick-line.md), then take each section in turn:
- **A pick is final**: that section is finished. Do what the profile says follows a pick (a port for a look page; a handoff note for /pick-enhance and /pick-expand), with the picked take's note and slider values. If the page gets another round (other tabs asked for changes), the picked tab stays exactly as it was and its take gets `picked: { round }`: nothing in that tab is removed, added or redrawn ([reference/pick-line.md](reference/pick-line.md#a-picked-tab-in-later-rounds)).
- **Requests without a pick** (Improve, Retry, Branch off, Combine, Drop, Drop branch, More takes) make the next round: steps A1–A5, with the line as the brief. A round with new sound takes also loads the `sound-design` skill, if it is installed, and runs its take plan and gate.
- **Ratings alone** ask for nothing: carry them into the next round. **A note that names a defect or says what to change** is a request even with no mark: see rule 3 in [reference/pick-line.md](reference/pick-line.md). When a line has nothing to act on, tell the author so in one line.

## Look pages (/pick-page)

- `kind: 'look'`: one pick per section. 4–8 takes after Now.
- Show the subject at the size and framing it really has, with what surrounds it, and add a close-up where detail matters. Controls cover its real variations.
- For anything already picked on an earlier page, reuse that page's painter code as it is (copy it into `src/`, naming its source).
- **Loop** (author, 6.11): the shell replays each take from t = 0 with its sound, so picture and sound always go together. A one-shot over motion that never stops (a burst over twinkling stars, a hit beside an idling ship) gets `len`, its clip's length; the shell finds every other end itself ([reference/shell.md](reference/shell.md#loop)).
- **Effects that last** (author, 11.11): build an effect from layers that end at different times, never one fade that takes it all out at once after a fifth of a second. A flash about 0.1 s; the shape that shows the reach 0.4–0.7 s; thrown pieces 0.7–1.3 s, each with its own random life (±30 %); a dim trace 1.5–2.5 s. Each layer holds, then fades. These layers start together: they are the take's rows (`layers`), not tabs. Effects on frequent hits stay shorter (0.5–0.9 s in all). A preview keeps the game's times; only the waits around an effect may be shorter. Before publishing, draw frames 0.5 s and 1 s after the impact: something is still there, its parts at different stages.
- **The player's avatar is not the effect** (author, 11.11): sell a cast or an impact with light and particles on and around it, never by scaling, kicking back, shaking or rolling what the player controls, unless the project's profile allows it. A skill that is a move (a dash) moves it, winding up in place.
- **Sounds** use the same page: each take plays from the panel (`audio`), at one master volume. Each variant is a different sound (another source or design), not one sound repitched or filtered. Check sounds with numbers and spectrograms, because the author judges by ear. A sound that plays over something (the fight, music, a room) names it in `under` and sends it into `X.under`, so **Solo** (S) plays the sound alone, without it, even mid-play (author, 5.11; [reference/shell.md](reference/shell.md#a-sounds-background)); the checker fails a tab whose background bypasses it. Each sound event the game plays apart (a launch, a flight, a hit) is its own tab in the thing's complex; the pieces of one clip (a wind-up, a slash, a tail) are the take's rows (`layers`), so the author rates and notes each on its own (author, 5.11; [reference/parts.md](reference/parts.md)). Tone variation gives every sound its own random pitch when it plays, as the game does. In a complex the author clicks the lanes, or presses Shift+R, to hear a late sound without the whole sequence before it (author, 11.11): every sound longer than one hit (each `whole` complex's sounds, a tab with several sounds, a held sound) starts mid-way from `X.from` and says `seek: true` ([reference/shell.md](reference/shell.md#a-start-on-the-lanes)).
